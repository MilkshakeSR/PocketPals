Pocket Pals 1.0.4

Fix a freeze at launch while Spotify or Music is open

Fixed

  • Pocket Pals could freeze at launch while Spotify or Music was open, leaving your pets standing still and the menu unresponsive. Pocket Pals no longer asks your music app anything directly; it only listens for the play and pause announcements those apps make, which need no permission. Music that was already playing when Pocket Pals opened is noticed at the next song.
