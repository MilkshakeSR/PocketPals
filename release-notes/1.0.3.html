Pocket Pals 1.0.3

Pets stay on their desktop

Fixed

  • Pets really do stay on their desktop now. They could turn up on another Space or over a fullscreen app after your Mac woke from sleep, a display changed, or you hid and showed them. Pocket Pals now waits until you're back on the pets' desktop before redrawing anything, and hiding pets fades them out in place instead of moving their window.
